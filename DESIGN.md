# Clarity interface
Mode: Operate. A calm reading workspace, with a dark evergreen navigation rail and warm paper content surface. A large Newsreader heading introduces each task; DM Sans handles controls, metadata and body copy. Subtle horizontal rules group clinical information without surrounding every field in a card.

Tokens: ink #203b36; muted #65716c; paper #f6f7f3; white #ffffff; accent #236653; accent-hover #194e3f; rail #163d38; line #dce2da; warning #795320 on #fff4df. Body 15px/1.6. Heading 42px desktop, 32px mobile. Controls have 8px radii, content panels 14px. Visible focus rings, reduced-motion support, minimum 44px action targets. No purely decorative charts or patient metrics.

Layout: 232px navigation rail, flexible main area with 1120px maximum content width. Input workspace and practical reading guidance share the desktop view; stack on mobile. Summary leads reports, clinical sections follow, with source disclosure per finding. History is a scan-friendly list with dates, status and summaries. Errors always name a recovery action.
